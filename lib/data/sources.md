# Sources (status as of Nov 1, 2026)

## OPENED and used
- Waite, *The Illustrated Key to the Tarot* (1910; text basis of *The Pictorial Key*, 1911). Project Gutenberg: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/43548 (txt: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/43548.txt.utf-8). Used for major arcana names, numbering and divinatory meanings.
- Golden Dawn, *Book T: The Tarot* (Mathers/Felkin text), archive.org transcription: https://archive.org/details/the-book-t-the-tarot. Used to verify Lord-of titles for 2–10, decan table, court titles and zodiac spans, and the Heh (Emperor) and Tzaddi (Star) attributions.
- Papus, *The Tarot of the Bohemians* (A.P. Morton translation), archive.org: https://archive.org/details/tarotofbohemians00papu_0. Only checked to confirm Papus follows Lévi's Hebrew-letter scheme (e.g. Moon = Tzaddi), which differs from the Golden Dawn. Not used for meanings.
- Alan Leo, *Astrology for All*, archive.org: https://archive.org/details/astrologyforall00leogoog. Used for sign polarity and rulers and its note on Neptune and Pisces.
- Culpeper, *The Complete Herbal*, Project Gutenberg: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/49513. Used for herb planetary rulers. A second scan (https://archive.org/details/b22009887) was downloaded, but its OCR is unusable.
- Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bursera_graveolens, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulnesia_sarmientoi (CITES App. II), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Leo, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermetic_Order_of_the_Golden_Dawn, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rider%E2%80%93Waite_Tarot

## NOT opened

- sacred-texts.com, hermetic.com (403); esotericarchives.com (404 or 500); IAPSOP (500).

- Scott Cunningham (copyrighted). Not fetched and not used; herb and stone correspondences are summarized from general folk tradition.
